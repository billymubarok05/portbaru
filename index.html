Digital Artist

My name is Brillian, but most people know me as Billy. I am an independent digital artist who enjoys creating character designs, mascots, and illustrated backgrounds.

My journey in the world of art actually began when I was a child. Even back then, my family noticed my interest and talent in drawing—or at least, according to them, I was pretty good at it. Well, parents are always your number-one supporters, right?

Because of that, my passion for art was fully supported. I started taking art classes at a very young age, continued throughout elementary school, and eventually attended an art school during my secondary education. In high school, I even took art preparation classes as a foundation for pursuing further education at university.

Eventually, I decided to study architecture. One of the reasons was that my drawing style at the time caught the attention of some of the instructors. Interestingly, my style back then was very free, rough, and unpolished—completely different from the various techniques I would later learn.

However, it was during this period that I gradually stopped painting. In fact, during some assessment sessions, we were not even allowed to use color in our drawings.

As time went by, my life took a different direction. I moved from place to place several times and worked as a photographer until the pandemic arrived.

Since the circumstances made it difficult for me to have enough space to create art traditionally as I used to, I decided to try something new: digital art. The desire to draw and paint again had become impossible to ignore.

However, starting with digital art was not as easy as I had imagined. The process felt completely different from what I was used to, and it was far less intuitive for me. On top of that, after spending years without consistently practicing my drawing skills, I felt that much of my ability had significantly declined.

It took me about a year of continuous learning, experimenting, failing, and trying again to gradually understand the new software and techniques. Through that long process, I discovered something I never expected: I completely fell in love with digital art.

There are still so many things I want to learn and countless ideas I want to bring to life through my work. So, if you're interested, I invite you to follow along and witness my journey as I continue to grow and develop as a digital artist.

P.S. I would also like to express my deepest gratitude to everyone who has supported and encouraged me to keep creating. I never imagined that this journey could grow as far as it has, especially with the help of social media.

The support from fellow artists, challenges, comments, and all the interactions I've experienced along the way have become an incredible source of inspiration for me. Many things I never imagined would happen have come to life through this process.

Thank you so much to everyone who has been a part of this journey.

Terms & Conditions — Brillian Illustration Commission

  • Harga dapat berubah tergantung pada tingkat detail, kebutuhan, dan kompleksitas permintaan.
  • Harga dasar berlaku untuk 1 karakter.

Simple Background

Background sederhana dapat berupa:

  • Warna flat/solid
  • Gradient
  • Bentuk atau pola sederhana
  • Garis horizon sederhana
  • Elemen dekoratif minimal
  • Sapuan brush yang soft, blurred, atau blended
  • Objek sederhana tanpa detail yang kompleks

Complex Background

Background kompleks dapat mencakup:

  • Beberapa layer dan depth untuk menciptakan ruang dan perspektif
  • Detail tekstur seperti dedaunan, bunga, kain, tanaman, dan sebagainya
  • Interior atau environment yang memiliki banyak detail
  • Objek tambahan
  • Hewan, burung, kupu-kupu, figur, dan subjek sekunder lainnya
  • Elemen environment yang membutuhkan proses rendering lebih detail

2. Commission Process & Delivery

Referensi sangat membantu saya dalam memahami dan mewujudkan ide yang kamu inginkan. Mohon berikan referensi sebanyak mungkin, terutama untuk:

Jika memiliki konsep khusus, semakin detail penjelasan yang diberikan akan semakin membantu proses pengerjaan.

Turnaround Time

Waktu pengerjaan commission biasanya sekitar 4 minggu. Namun, waktu pengerjaan dapat lebih lama tergantung pada:

  • Kompleksitas artwork
  • Jumlah karakter
  • Detail background
  • Jumlah revisi
  • Antrian commission
  • Kecepatan klien dalam memberikan feedback

Saya akan memberikan update selama proses pengerjaan berlangsung.

Commission Stages

  1. Discussion & Quotation: Kita akan membahas request, kebutuhan, dan detail artwork. Setelah itu saya akan memberikan estimasi harga.
  2. Approval & Payment: Setelah kamu menyetujui harga dan detail commission, invoice akan dikirimkan. Pengerjaan akan dimulai setelah pembayaran yang telah disepakati diterima.
  3. Sketch: Saya akan membuat sketch berdasarkan konsep yang telah disepakati. Pada tahap ini, kamu dapat memberikan feedback mengenai: Pose, Komposisi, Proporsi, Ekspresi, Elemen utama lainnya.
  4. Lineart: Setelah sketch disetujui, artwork akan dilanjutkan ke lineart. Mohon periksa dengan teliti pada tahap ini karena perubahan besar setelah lineart disetujui dapat dikenakan biaya tambahan.
  5. Flat Colors: Warna dasar akan diberikan dan dikirimkan untuk approval. Perubahan warna kecil masih dapat dilakukan pada tahap ini.
  6. Final Rendering: Setelah flat colors disetujui dan pembayaran penuh telah diterima, artwork akan masuk ke tahap final rendering. Pada tahap ini hanya perubahan kecil yang diperbolehkan.

File Delivery

Final artwork akan dikirimkan dalam bentuk file digital melalui email. Tidak ada produk fisik yang dikirimkan kecuali telah disepakati sebelumnya.

4. Revisions

Maksimal 3 kali revisi tersedia selama tahap sketch, lineart, dan flat colors.

  • Sketch: Perubahan besar maupun kecil masih diperbolehkan selama tidak mengubah keseluruhan konsep yang telah disepakati.
  • Lineart: Perubahan besar masih dapat dilakukan, tetapi sebaiknya disampaikan sebelum lineart disetujui.
  • Flat Colors: Perubahan warna kecil masih diperbolehkan. Perubahan besar terhadap konsep, pose, atau struktur artwork setelah tahap ini dapat dikenakan biaya tambahan.
  • Final Rendering: Pada tahap final rendering, hanya minor adjustments yang diperbolehkan (contohnya: penyesuaian warna kecil, perubahan detail kecil, koreksi minor pada elemen tertentu).

Perubahan besar seperti mengubah pose, ekspresi, hairstyle, outfit, komposisi utama, atau menggambar ulang bagian besar artwork dapat dikenakan additional revision fee. Biaya revisi akan disesuaikan dengan tingkat kesulitan perubahan.

Saya ingin hasil akhirnya sesuai dengan apa yang kamu bayangkan. Jadi, jangan ragu untuk menyampaikan feedback selama proses berlangsung. Komunikasi yang jelas akan sangat membantu kita mendapatkan hasil terbaik. ♡
